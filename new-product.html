Product Market Fit Scope

Is Your Product Ready for the Market?

Before launching a product, it is equally important to check whether it solves a customer pain point to make it a success.

What is Product-Market Fit Analysis?

Product Market Fit:

  • Analyzes whether the product solves a real buyer problem
  • Studies the product in detail and identifies the market demand for it.
  • Evaluates present alternatives in the same market segment to find out if any competitive advantage exists.
  • Recommends the positioning and strategy needed to lower entry barriers.

Why it Matters for New Products?

  • You risk building a product buyers don't actually want, and fixing that after launch costs far more than fixing it before.
  • You risk spending on positioning and growth for a product with no real demand behind it.
  • You risk entering a crowded market with nothing that sets you apart from competitors.
  • Reduce adoption barriers by positioning the product around real buyer needs from day one.
